# Orders

## Placing orders

Use `create_order` to place an order for a customer.

## Cancelling orders

An order can be called off free of charge until it leaves the warehouse.

## Shipping

Parcels are sent by standard post, and express delivery costs extra.

## Returns

Unused goods can be sent back within 30 days.

## Order history

Customers see their past purchases with the most recent at the top.
